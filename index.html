01 / WHO WE ARE

About
our school

A welcoming place for learning from Class 9 through Class 12, serving students across secondary and higher secondary levels.

“

PM SHRI Government Boys Higher Secondary School Laderwan is a school community focused on learning, curiosity and the growth of every student.

Our school serves students in Classes 9–12, with Secondary classes in 9–10 and Higher Secondary classes in 11–12. This portal brings together useful school information for students, families, staff and alumni.

School details and announcements are published by the school administration. Information that has not been confirmed by the school is intentionally left out.
