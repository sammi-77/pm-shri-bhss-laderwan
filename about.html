About
our school

A co-educational school serving Classes IX–XII in Laderwan, Kupwara, with a history of learning and community service dating back to 1943.

“

BHSS Laderwan is a community school committed to helping students learn, think critically, grow with strong values, and prepare for higher education and responsible citizenship.

Established by the Department of Education in 1943 on land donated by Mohd Yousuf Wani, the school was upgraded to middle school in 1990 and attained higher secondary status in 2019.

Serving students from Classes IX–XII, BHSS Laderwan is affiliated with the Jammu & Kashmir Board of School Education (JKBOSE). Its academic streams include Science (Medical and Non-Medical) and Arts.

Our campus

The campus includes a library with more than 500 books, electricity, maintained classrooms, playground facilities, and spaces for cricket, volleyball, football, and other sports. It has separate toilets for boys and girls, accessibility ramps, and cafeteria facilities. The school reports 24/7 CCTV surveillance. A dedicated computer lab is not yet available; technology facilities are being developed.

Learning & community

The school partners with national NGOs and local organizations to support educational opportunities, share resources, and strengthen community well-being. BHSS Laderwan has also participated in a drug-awareness program involving students, teachers, and parents from the Trehgam zone.

School profile

The supplied profile lists approximately 29 teachers (24 male and 5 female) and a student–teacher ratio of about 30:1. It also notes smaller batches of around 60 students in recent years and enrollment exceeding 890 across Grades IX–XII. BHSS Laderwan serves as the only higher secondary institution within several kilometers.
